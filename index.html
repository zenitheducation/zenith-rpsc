The Hard Truth

Attempting RPSC isn't enough — first, you must cross the qualifying line.

RPSC's selection journey begins at a minimum qualifying mark — not on exam day. Most aspirants miss the merit because they stumble at this line.

Cross the line first. Then race for merit.

As per RPSC's selection criteria, candidates must secure a minimum of 40% (General) and 35% (reserved categories, with 5% relaxation) — only then does the merit cut-off begin. If your existing material hasn't carried you past this line yet, repeating it will not change the outcome.

Change the material — and the result will change. Proven, exam-trend-aligned notes turn near-misses into confirmed selections.
